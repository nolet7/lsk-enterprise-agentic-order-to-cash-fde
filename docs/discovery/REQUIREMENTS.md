## Exclusions until specifically approved
No automatic supplier approval, contract-term negotiation, payment capture, credit issuing, inventory reservation, regulatory interpretation, or uncapped AI-generated actions. Regional legal obligations and certificate types must be determined with the client's qualified compliance team. Synthetic examples cannot establish compliance.

## Stage-wise definition of done
- POC: exercised synthetic scenarios with implementation links and actual test output; proposed supplier integration may be mocked, clearly tagged.
- Pilot: signed contracts and UAT; negative tests for tenant isolation, expired certificates and human override; metric baselines collected.
- Production: independent security/compliance review, reconciled live adapters, recovery drills, SLO/error budgets, on-call ownership, documented approval and release evidence.
